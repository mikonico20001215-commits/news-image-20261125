A one-image summary of Wednesday 25 November 2026's world news, drawn as a
newspaper front page: the date across the masthead, six stories in panels
with a short label each, and a ticker of three more stories along the foot.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

## What the image shows

1. **TWO TANKERS HIT BY MINES IN HORMUZ** — Iran's Revolutionary Guard
   says two oil tankers struck naval mines and caught fire early on
   Wednesday while using an unauthorised route through the strait.
2. **HUAWEI FOUND GUILTY IN U.S. COURT** — A Brooklyn jury convicts the
   Chinese tech firm on 10 of 12 counts, including bank fraud and
   trade-secret theft; it is cleared of racketeering.
3. **UKRAINE DRONES HIT TWO REFINERIES** — Drones hit Rosneft's Syzran
   and Kuibyshev refineries in Russia's Samara region overnight, setting
   both ablaze; one person is killed.
4. **PAKISTAN'S FIRST ASTRONAUT IN ORBIT** — China's Shenzhou-24 carries
   Pakistan's first astronaut into space and docks with the Tiangong
   station about three and a half hours after launch.
5. **AUSTRALIA SIGNS PACIFIC SECURITY PACT** — Australia and Solomon
   Islands sign a treaty in Canberra barring any foreign military or
   security base on the islands.
6. **19 CIVILIANS KILLED IN SUDAN DRONE STRIKES** — Sudan's paramilitary
   Rapid Support Forces kill 19 people and wound 20 in drone strikes on
   two cities, rights lawyers say.

Ticker: Houthis say they hit the Ras Tanura refinery in Saudi Arabia; the
Dow closes above 50,000 for the first time; Colombia frees all hostages in
the Cartagena supermarket siege.

## How it was made

The image (`entry.png`, 1024×1024 PNG) was composed as a page of flat
illustrations and typeset text drawn for this entry — no photographs or
artwork copied from anywhere — and rendered to PNG. `main.ts` (Node 22,
standard library only) checks the PNG against the gate (whole PNG, at most
1 MiB, at most 1024×1024) and copies it to `/output/entry.png`.
